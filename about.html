About the Red River Métis Shoebox Archive

Red River Métis National Heritage Centre

What the Shoebox Archive is

The Red River Métis Shoebox Archive is a community photo archive. For generations, Métis families kept their photographs in shoeboxes, dresser drawers, and photo albums. Those photographs hold family faces, gatherings, homesteads, and the everyday life of the Red River Métis from the 1800s to today.

The archive gathers those photographs in one place, records the stories that go with them, and makes them available to the community, researchers, and the public. Every photo is kept with the name of the family who shared it, and every contribution is guided by the consent of the people who gave it.

How to take part

Share your photos

Photos of family, community events, homesteads, or everyday life are welcome. Digital copies are preferred, but original photos can also be scanned at a Shoebox table.

Sign consent and submit photos

Tell the story

The people, places, dates, and memories attached to a photo are what make it part of the community's story. Every detail, even a name or a year, helps.

Visit a Shoebox table

Shoebox tables appear at Métis Nation events across the homeland. Bring photos to be scanned, or just come and look through what has been shared.

Contact

Questions about the archive, consent, or a photo already in the collection can be sent to metisshoebox@mmf.mb.ca.

The archive is a project of the Red River Métis National Heritage Centre with the Manitoba Métis Federation.
