Contribute to the Red River Métis Shoebox Archive

Red River Métis National Heritage Centre

Step 1: Sign the consent form

The Shoebox Archive preserves Métis family photographs and the stories behind them. Before we can accept and display your photos, we need your consent in writing. The form below takes a few minutes. It explains how your photos will be used, stored, and shared.

Open the consent form in a new tab
If the form does not load here, use the button above to open it in a new tab. A copy of your signed form is sent to you by email when you finish.

Step 2: Send us your photos

Once your consent form is signed, you can send us your photographs. The online photo upload form is on its way. Until it arrives, you can email your photos to metisshoebox@mmf.mb.ca, or bring them to a Shoebox table at an event near you.

If you can, tell us who is in each photo, where it was taken, and roughly when. Every detail helps us tell the story.
