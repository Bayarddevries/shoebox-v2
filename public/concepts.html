Attract screen concepts

Four directions for the pedestal tablet's idle screen (1280x800). All of them: never crop a photo (every print keeps its own aspect ratio), warm archival palette, wordmark plus a quiet "touch to explore" cue, and only one cheap motion each so the tablet stays fast.

Concept A
The Wall
Justified photo-book rows, aligned edge to edge, generous gutters. One slow light sweep across the wall. Ordered, archival, calm. 25 prints.
Concept B
The Luminous Field
Prints floating in a dark warm void, each in its own halo of light, with a clear focal centre. Drama from light, not movement. Ethereal.
Concept C
The Portrait Wall
Three large faces with names as anchors, a field of smaller prints around them. Hierarchy: faces entice, the field shows the archive's depth.
Concept D
The Spill
Prints laid on a warm lit surface as if the box was just tipped over: slight rotations, gentle overlap, soft shadows. Warm and human.

Hero pool (80 candidates)
The shortlist the concepts draw from. Cut anything that doesn't belong and I'll rebuild the pool.
One honest trade-off. At 1280x800, showing 25 prints means each is about 130px tall (roughly 1.2 inches on a 10-inch tablet): great for signalling "there's a lot here", weak at pulling someone in. Showing 9 prints gives each about 330px (3 inches): much stronger emotional pull, less sense of range. Concepts above sit at different points on that scale so you can feel which matters more.
