Contribute to the Red River Métis Shoebox Archive

Red River Métis National Heritage Centre

The Shoebox Archive preserves Métis family photographs and the stories behind them. Submitting photos takes about ten minutes and happens all in one form: consent first, then your details, then your photos and what you know about them.

1

Sign consent

The form starts with the consent form. It takes a few minutes and explains how photos are used, stored, and shared. A copy of the signed form is sent by email when it is finished.

2

Add your details

Name, contact information, and, if you like, your family or community name and MMF member number. This stays with the archive, not the public site.

3

Upload your photos and their stories

Choose up to 20 photos. For each one, add what you know: who is in it, where it was taken, roughly when, any occasion, and the memory you want to keep with it. Details are welcome but not required.

Continue to the consent and upload form
Prefer email? Send photos to metisshoebox@mmf.mb.ca, or bring them to a Shoebox table at an event.
