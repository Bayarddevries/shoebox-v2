Contribute to the Red River Métis Shoebox Archive

Red River Métis National Heritage Centre

Step 1: Sign the consent form

The Shoebox Archive preserves Métis family photographs and the stories behind them. Before photos can be accepted and displayed, consent is needed in writing. The form below takes a few minutes and explains how photos will be used, stored, and shared.

Open the consent form in a new tab
If the form does not load here, use the button above to open it in a new tab. A copy of the signed form is sent by email when the form is finished.

Step 2: Upload your photos

Once the consent form is signed, use the form below (or the button) to send photos and the details that go with them: who is in each photo, where it was taken, roughly when, and any memory worth keeping.

Continue to the photo upload form

The upload form opens in this window and walks through the same steps: confirm consent, add your details, choose your photos, and tell us what you know about each one.

Prefer email? Send photos to metisshoebox@mmf.mb.ca, or bring them to a Shoebox table at an event.
