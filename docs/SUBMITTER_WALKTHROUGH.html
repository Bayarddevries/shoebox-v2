How to help document your photo

You're not on a test. The link lets you see your photos, add names, places, stories, and dates so the archive can hold them properly. No account needed.

For each picture, fill in what you know

People (left to right) — names and how they relate to you (e.g., "Margaret Lapointe, mother", "Uncle Joseph").
Location — town, community, reserve, or region. "Winnipeg — St. Boniface" is fine; "near the church in St. Vital" is fine too.
Approx. year / era — "around 1952", "early 1940s", "before the flood" — anything that helps.
Occasion / event — harvest, wedding, church gathering, meeting, school day, family visit.
Story — a few sentences about what was happening, why it matters, anything you want future people to know.
Caption — a short line describing the photo (what you want others to see when they browse it).
Attribution / credit — "photo by me", "courtesy of Aunt Rose", or leave blank.

What if you don't know everything?

Write what you do. "Not sure of the year — maybe 1960s" is better than leaving it blank. "Some of the people — I can ask later" is fine. The archive can hold partial records; they get improved over time — they don't have to be perfect first.

About your contact info

When you submit, you can include or skip email / phone. It lets the archive reach you if a story is added later — never for anything else. You control it.

After you submit

The archive admin reviews each contribution before it joins the archive. You can come back to update details any time through your private link.
