# Shoebox Governance (DRAFT STUB)

Status: placeholder. Being developed as part of the community-governance
workstream (2026-09). Nothing here is operative until reviewed with the
community advisory body and signed off.

## North star

A world-class photo archive **for** the Red River Métis people, held **in
trust** by the MMF/RRMNHC. Community owns the data, decides access, and
possesses the masters (OCAP-aligned).

## Planned structure

- **Community advisory body** (Elders + youth + staff) — charter and
  membership TBD. Advises on: what is shown, sensitive/deceased-person
  handling, cultural protocols, naming, takedown disputes.
- **Decision rights** — who approves display of sensitive content, who
  executes takedowns (project lead, escalate to RRMNHC director), who
  authorizes external use.
- **Access matrix** — repo, Apps Script project, Google Sheet, master disk,
  consent form custody; what happens when each person leaves.
- **Cultural protocols** — deceased-person display, ceremonial content,
  residential-school-era warnings, community-defined "do not show"
  categories. To be co-developed with Elders (see cultural_protocols.md).

## Open items

- Advisory body membership + charter (needs leadership sign-off, possibly honoraria)
- Accountability to citizens whose photos are archived
- French/Michif naming and content decisions
